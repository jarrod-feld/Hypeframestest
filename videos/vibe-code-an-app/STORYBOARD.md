## Video direction

- **Palette system (from `frame.md` — BlockFrame):** `offwhite`/`cream` are the canvas grounds; `black` is ink — all text, 4px borders, 8px hard offset shadows. One pastel owns each act as its section color: `pink` for the hook/concept act (Frames 1–2), `yellow` for the build steps (3–7, chips + accents), `blue` for the marketing act (8–10), `green` for the money/win beats (11), `black` ground for the recap (12), back to `offwhite` for the CTA (13). Star bursts, label pills, and tilted decorations are BlockFrame chrome — use per frame, sparingly. STEP/MOVE chips share one identity (label-pill, top-left) across the whole video — they are the step rail.
- **Type:** display ramp is Inter 800–900 uppercase (heading-lg / quote-text / stat-number roles); labels and counters are Space Grotesk uppercase. Type is the primary visual — heroes near full-bleed.
- **Motion grammar + reveal model:** smooth long-tail settles, `power3` default — no bouncy entrances. Every frame reveals each piece **on its spoken cue**, spreading arrivals across the back ~50%; nothing front-loads. Spring overshoot is rationed to one earned pop per frame at most (a badge, a star burst, the final CTA line) — BlockFrame is playful, but the playfulness lives in the shapes, not in bounce. During holds: stillness; at most subtle jitter. Internal seams are velocity-matched cuts.
- **Rhythm / held frames:** Frame 8 ends on a deliberately dead-still held read (the desert tension before the marketing act); Frame 12 holds its full recap stack; Frame 13's final line holds to the end. Everything else keeps revealing to the VO.
- **Negative list:** no bouncy/`back.out` default entrances; no lazy breathing; no back-half pans or pushes; no slideshow (front-load-then-freeze); no screensaver (independent floaters); no purple-blue "AI" gradients; no browser chrome, navbars, or real cursors — the two sanctioned interface mocks are Frame 4's typed input card and Frame 7's DEPLOY button/cursor, because the topic at that moment *is* that interface; no photoreal imagery — everything is BlockFrame flat pictogram + type.
- **Captions:** plan all content into the top ~83%; bottom band stays clear. Centered heroes anchor at y ≈ 0.42 × height.
