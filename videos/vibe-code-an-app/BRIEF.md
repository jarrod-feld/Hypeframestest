## Intent

A ~2 minute faceless explainer that teaches a total beginner, in five-year-old-simple
language, how to build an app end to end with a vibe coding tool: describe the app in
plain words, let the AI pick the tech stack (with a simple default recipe), click
deploy to launch, then market it — start free (share where your people hang out),
then test paid ads with a small budget, keep what works. Friendly, playful,
teacher-at-a-whiteboard energy. No jargon without an instant kid-level translation.

## Customizations

- Open-source TTS narration (user asked for it explicitly) — Kokoro local engine, offline.
- Full journey in one video: idea → tech stack → build → launch → marketing strategy
  (organic first, then paid ads with a test budget).
